PTCG Snap Redeem Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

Overview

PTCG Snap Redeem helps users capture visible QR codes from YouTube videos and open the official Pokémon TCG Live redemption page with a decoded code.

Data handling

The extension processes the visible image of the active YouTube tab locally in the browser to decode a QR code. QR image data and decoded codes are not sent to the developer or to any third-party server by the extension.

Decoded codes are temporarily copied to the device clipboard and saved in the browser's local extension storage to support the extension's functionality. Users may clear extension data by removing the extension from Chrome.

Third-party websites

After a successful scan, the extension opens the official Pokémon TCG Live redemption website. That website is operated by its owner and is governed by its own privacy policy and terms.

Data sales and sharing

PTCG Snap Redeem does not sell, rent, transfer, or use user data for advertising, profiling, creditworthiness, or any purpose unrelated to its QR redemption workflow.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the publisher through the Chrome Web Store support channel.
